Support Contribution Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Springs Local Small Businesses is free for customers and local businesses to use. The website offers optional support contributions to help cover basic hosting, maintenance, and future directory improvements.

Optional contributions

Support contributions are voluntary. A contribution is not required to search the directory, create an account, apply for a listing, or use the directory’s free features.

Not tax-deductible

Contributions are not charitable donations and are not tax-deductible.

Payment processing

Contributions are processed securely by Stripe. Springs Local Small Businesses does not store full payment-card information on this website.

Refund requests

If you made a contribution by mistake or have a payment concern, contact us within 30 days of the payment. Include the name used for payment, the approximate date, the amount, and the email address associated with the transaction. Refund requests are reviewed individually.

Contact

For support-contribution or refund questions, email springsbusinesses@gmail.com.
