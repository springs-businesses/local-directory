Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Springs Local Small Businesses is a Colorado Springs directory intended to help customers find local small businesses and help local businesses apply to be listed. This Privacy Policy explains the information we collect, how we use it, and how to contact us with questions.

Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide when you use the directory, including:

  • Your name, email address, and account information when you create a customer account.
  • Saved search information connected to your customer account.
  • Business application information, including business name, contact name, email, phone number, website, service area, services offered, and verification details.
  • Messages or information you send when contacting the directory.

How we use information

We use submitted information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the directory.
  • Create and manage customer accounts and saved searches.
  • Review business applications and publish approved business listings.
  • Respond to questions, correction requests, complaints, and support requests.
  • Protect the directory from spam, fraud, misuse, or security issues.

Public business listings

If a business application is approved, information selected for the public listing may be visible to anyone using the directory. This can include the business name, service area, services, business description, website, social-media link, business email, and business phone number.

Payment processing

Optional support contributions are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store full payment-card numbers on this website. Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy policies and terms.

Service providers

We use third-party services to operate the website, including website hosting, database and account services, forms, and payment processing. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information submitted through the directory. No online system can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the directory, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

Your choices

You may request to update or remove a public business listing, ask questions about your information, or request account-related assistance by contacting us. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email: springsbusinesses@gmail.com

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the directory changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was most recently revised.
